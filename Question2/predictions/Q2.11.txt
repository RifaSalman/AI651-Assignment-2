Model B should remove the mirror and rotation failures. Those errors stay well below the blank predictor: transformed E(5) is only a little higher than the original E(5), so this looks like a missing symmetry rather than a broken motion rule, and B is the only one of these three models that sees g drawn from G. Model C should be the one that repairs 2×, because twice the median speed is 6.6 pixels, inside the 8-pixel reach, and C is the first model that actually sees k = 2; it should not repair 3×, where a step of about 10 pixels is beyond that reach and E(2) is already worse than blank. Model D keeps C’s speed augmentation and extends the reach to 16 pixels, so it is the model that should bring 3× back, while the mirror and rotation gaps stay until the forecaster is symmetrised.
